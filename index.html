KIN = your people.

Rachel landed in Australia knowing nobody. A workout changed that.

At a wellness event, she was paired with someone for the workout. They became friends, and stayed friends. At another class, it happened again.

Those connections changed her experience of a place that was completely new to her. Movement gave them something to do together. Being introduced gave them somewhere to start.

Now, Rachel is bringing that feeling home. As a personal trainer and Pilates instructor, she knows how to lead a class. With KIN, she wants to make meeting people feel just as much a part of it.

Can I come on my own?

Yes. KIN is designed with solo arrivals in mind. Rachel will welcome you and introduce you to another guest before class. You don’t need to find someone to come with you.

Who is KIN for?

Anyone who wants to meet people through movement. Maybe you’ve just moved here. Maybe you’ve lived here for years and would like a few new faces in your week.

Will I be doing Pilates with a partner?

No. The pairing is for the welcome, not the workout. You’ll have your own mat and follow Rachel’s class at your own pace.

Do I have to join every conversation?

No. Introductions are an invitation. You can take your time, skip a prompt, or leave after class. There’s no pressure to share anything personal.

What if I’m new to Pilates?

You’re welcome. Pilates at the Patch is a 45-minute mat class for all levels, and Rachel will offer options as she goes. Mats are provided, so just bring yourself and a water bottle.

When is the next event?

Pilates at the Patch is next: a mat Pilates class at 1818 Farm and Cidery, with a cider tasting after. It’s on Saturday, October 24 at 10am and costs $35. See the event details.

Is there a non-alcoholic option?

Not at this event. The tasting after class is cider only. You’re welcome to join the conversation without a glass.
